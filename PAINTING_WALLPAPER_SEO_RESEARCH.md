## 9. Keyword mapping by page type

### Interior location page

- Primary: best local form, e.g. `interior house painting sacramento`.
- Secondary: `interior painters {city}`.
- Supporting: interior painting contractors, services, residential interior painting, walls, quote/estimate, cost.
- One page should cover these close service synonyms; do not create a separate page for each.

### Exterior location page

- Primary: best local form, e.g. `exterior house painting san diego`.
- Secondary: `exterior painters {city}`.
- Supporting: contractors, services, residential exterior painting, home painting, quote/estimate, cost.

### Wallpaper location page

- Primary: `wallpaper installer {city}` or the highest-volume local variant.
- Secondary: `wallpaper installation {city}`.
- Supporting: wallpaper hanger, wallpaper hanging, professional installer, peel-and-stick installer, installer cost.
- Do not target wallpaper products, phone wallpapers, desktop backgrounds, colors, patterns, or stores.

## 10. Page template

Every service-location page should include:

1. City-specific H1 and direct estimate CTA.
2. Unique local introduction.
3. Exact service scope and exclusions.
4. Surface/preparation process relevant to the service.
5. Interior rooms and wall types, exterior siding/stucco surfaces, or wallpaper material types as applicable.
6. Estimate/cost factors without invented fixed prices.
7. Scheduling and preparation expectations.
8. Verified ZIP codes, neighborhoods, housing context, climate/material considerations, and permit rules where relevant.
9. Four to six unique FAQs.
10. Links to the service hub and nearby pages for the same service.
11. Visible business NAP/phone only after the second site's real information is supplied.
12. Service and Breadcrumb schema using verified fields.

Do not publish city-name substitutions. Each page needs at least three verified local details and genuinely unique copy.

## 12. Excluded keyword noise

Exclude from service SEO:

- DIY/how-to queries;
- paint products, brands, colors, samples, tools, brushes, rollers, sprayers;
- paintings/art/canvas/paint-and-sip;
- automotive, boats, miniatures, face/body/nail painting;
- jobs, salaries, training, courses, licenses;
- phone, desktop, 4K, live, aesthetic, character, and seasonal wallpapers;
- wallpaper products/stores unless the second business actually sells products;
- cabinet, furniture, fence, deck, roof, floor, commercial, mural, removal, and repair queries unless added to scope.
