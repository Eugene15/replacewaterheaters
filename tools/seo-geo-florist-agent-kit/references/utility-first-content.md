# Utility-first landing-page content

Use SEO as an acquisition constraint, not as the page's reason to exist. Build the page around what the visitor must understand, decide, believe or do and what verified evidence, useful information or working capability can help.

## Mandatory upstream artifacts

Before page strategy or architecture, produce and validate:

1. `competitor-function-analysis.json`: map competitor elements to the buyer concern, functional job and proof type. Never copy their wording, section order or trust architecture.
2. `evidence-inventory.json`: separate `availableVerified`, `conditional`, `unavailableOrUnverified`, and `desiredCapabilities`. Missing evidence may reveal a product opportunity but never authorizes substitute marketing language.
3. `visitor-state.json`: record trigger states, knowledge gaps, anxieties, decisions, action barriers and the minimum useful next step.

## Architecture test

For every proposed section record:

- user question or concern;
- useful information delivered;
- reason the section deserves to exist;
- supporting evidence IDs;
- intended action or decision;
- contribution: `understand`, `decide`, `trust`, or `act`;
- what would be lost if the section were removed.

Reject a section whose main justification is SEO, competitor precedent, city-name insertion or page length. Local sections require verified local information such as an authority, permit path, utility, rebate, regulation, supported housing condition or service logistics.

## Trust model

Prefer process transparency and decision support over unverified reputation claims. Do not imply history, popularity, experience, reviews, awards, workmanship, customer sentiment or local standing without canonical fact IDs. When the company lacks proof for a concern, state the gap internally and either provide verified process utility or omit the claim.

## Transactional utility

Treat the page as the beginning of the service, not an article with a CTA appended. Prefer useful actions such as identifying current equipment, collecting the minimum photos or details, comparing viable paths, explaining scope and cost drivers, qualifying timing, or preparing a request. Mention only capabilities that exist and are verified.

## Section utility critic

After copy and before SEO, classify every section with one or more of:

- `USER_VALUE`
- `DECISION_SUPPORT`
- `TRUST`
- `LOCAL_VALUE`
- `CONVERSION`
- `VERIFIED_PROOF`
- `GENERIC_FILLER`
- `SEO_ONLY`
- `UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM`

`GENERIC_FILLER`, `SEO_ONLY` and `UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM` block progression. Delete or return the section to its owner. The critic must also flag unnecessary city-name insertion and generic service-business language whose removal loses no concrete information.

## Quality bar

Ask: if this page ranked first tomorrow, would it genuinely be one of the most useful pages for a buyer facing this problem? Technical SEO completeness cannot override a failed utility answer.

## Approved directional example for water-heater GEO pages

Use the Roseville page example in `Rework the SEO - Landing Page Content Agent.md` as the approved content-direction reference for this project. Learn its functional pattern:

- open from the homeowner's trigger: no hot water, leak, or planned replacement;
- let a visitor start without knowing technical terminology;
- request concrete minimum inputs, including useful heater and installation photos;
- explain cost through equipment and site variables instead of unsupported generic prices;
- support the like-for-like versus tankless versus heat-pump decision honestly;
- introduce local permit or utility facts only where verified and decision-relevant;
- explain what happens after the request;
- place useful next actions where the visitor has enough context to act.

Do not clone its headings, section count, order, claims, ZIP codes, permit statements or CTA capabilities onto another route. Reuse the reasoning model and block functions; architecture must still come from that route's visitor state, evidence inventory and local evidence.

Before UI implementation, compare the proposed semantic-role sequence, component sequence, hero pattern, section density and CTA placement with adjacent GEO pages. A page that transfers the example's composition rather than its reasoning fails the diversity gate as a templated recipe.
