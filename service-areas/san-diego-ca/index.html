Do I need a permit for water-heater replacement in San Diego?

For a single-family home or duplex, the City of San Diego lists water heater replacement as Simple MEP work: a permit is required, but plans are not required for the basic scope. The city separately says a tank-to-tankless conversion requires plans and plan review. Confirm the property jurisdiction and current requirements before work begins.

Can the existing tank be changed to tankless?

Possibly, but it is not automatically an in-kind replacement. Fuel or electrical capacity, venting, power, drainage, clearances, location, and the local review path must be checked.

What information helps with an estimate?

The exact address, property type, heater label, equipment and fuel type, wide installation photos, access constraints, and your preferred replacement path make the initial review more specific.

Can replacement be completed the same day?

Call before noon. Completion by evening may be possible, but it depends on the day’s route, equipment availability, site conditions, and permit requirements. Availability must be confirmed by phone.
