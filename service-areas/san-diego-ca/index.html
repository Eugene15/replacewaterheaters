Does a San Diego replacement require plans?

City guidance lists basic water-heater replacement as Simple MEP work for a single-family home or duplex: a permit is required, but plans are not required for that basic scope. Property type and jurisdiction still need confirmation.

What changes for tank-to-tankless conversion?

San Diego guidance says that conversion requires plans and plan review. The site also needs review for fuel or electrical capacity, venting, condensate, clearances, and mounting.

What should I send before calling?

Send the exact address, property type, heater label, wide and close photos, current fuel, access route, and whether you want the same type or a conversion.

Can it be completed today?

Call before noon to ask. Completion by evening may be possible, but the route, equipment, site conditions, and permit requirements must be confirmed by phone.
