Estimate inputs

Describe the current system, requested change, and property conditions.

For a compatible tank, the useful comparison starts with the existing capacity, fuel, footprint, water connections, vent or circuit, drain arrangement, and the route used to remove the unit. A conversion adds a different set of unknowns: available gas or electrical capacity, manufacturer clearances, mounting surface, condensate routing, exhaust path, and service access. Photos support the first review but do not establish that the installation is code-compliant. Property type and jurisdiction also matter because the cited Simple MEP description is limited to the city guidance and the stated residential context. These inputs explain scope without relying on an unsupported flat price or a citywide timing promise.

Existing system
Fuel, capacity, label, location, connections, venting, drainage, seismic restraint, and access.
Requested change
Keep the type, convert to tankless or heat pump, change fuel, or move the installation.
Property conditions
Exact address, building type, clearances, finished surfaces, removal route, and permit context.

Conversion questions

San Diego answers before equipment selection.

Does a San Diego replacement require plans?

City guidance lists basic water-heater replacement as Simple MEP work for a single-family home or duplex: a permit is required, but plans are not required for that basic scope. Property type and jurisdiction still need confirmation.

What changes for tank-to-tankless conversion?

San Diego guidance says that conversion requires plans and plan review. The site also needs review for fuel or electrical capacity, venting, condensate, clearances, and mounting.

What should I send before calling?

Send the exact address, property type, heater label, wide and close photos, current fuel, access route, and whether you want the same type or a conversion.

Can it be completed today?

Call before noon to ask. Completion by evening may be possible, but the route, equipment, site conditions, and permit requirements must be confirmed by phone.
