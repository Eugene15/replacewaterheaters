Is an online permit path available in San Jose?

The city describes an online path for qualifying like-for-like gas tank replacement. Different equipment, a new location, or broader work can require another review path.

Could an electrical permit be needed?

Yes. San Jose states that replacement requires a plumbing permit and that an electrical permit may also be required, depending on the equipment and work.

Do earthquake straps matter?

Yes. San Jose requires water heaters to be braced and strapped for earthquake resistance. Photos of both straps help clarify the existing setup.
