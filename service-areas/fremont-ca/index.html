What does Fremont list under Residential Plumbing?

Fremont lists repair, alteration, or same-location replacement of an existing tank, tankless, or heat-pump water heater under its Residential Plumbing permit category.

Where does Fremont direct basic replacement permits?

The city directs basic home-upgrade permits, including water-heater replacement, through Citizen Access. Current eligibility should be confirmed for the exact project.

What if the heater moves?

A move to another wall, room, garage area, or exterior location is not the same-location scope. Connections, drainage, venting, utilities, clearances, and permit routing need a separate review.

Can it be completed today?

Call before noon to ask. Completion by evening depends on the route, equipment, verified site conditions, and permit requirements and must be confirmed by phone.
