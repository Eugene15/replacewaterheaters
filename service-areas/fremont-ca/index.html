Why the location answer matters

Fremont’s published category specifically names same-location replacement. That makes the proposed placement a useful scope boundary, not a decorative question. If the heater moves, show the proposed wall or room as well as the old location. A move can affect the water route, gas or electrical work, venting, drainage, clearances, seismic restraint, finished surfaces, and removal access.

For a system-type change, include the desired equipment and any available panel, circuit, gas, vent, airflow, or condensate information. Citizen Access is the city’s portal direction for basic upgrades, but the exact permit route still depends on the verified project.

Fremont replacement questions

Use the answer that matches the location decision.

What does Fremont list under Residential Plumbing?

Fremont lists repair, alteration, or same-location replacement of an existing tank, tankless, or heat-pump water heater under its Residential Plumbing permit category.

Where does Fremont direct basic replacement permits?

The city directs basic home-upgrade permits, including water-heater replacement, through Citizen Access. Current eligibility should be confirmed for the exact project.

What if the heater moves?

A move to another wall, room, garage area, or exterior location is not the same-location scope. Connections, drainage, venting, utilities, clearances, and permit routing need a separate review.

Can it be completed today?

Call before noon to ask. Completion by evening depends on the schedule, equipment, verified site conditions, and permit requirements and must be confirmed by phone.
