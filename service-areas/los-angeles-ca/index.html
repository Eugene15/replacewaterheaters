Los Angeles FAQ

Answers for this project path.

All service areasCalifornia replacement
Does Los Angeles require a permit?

City guidance says installation or replacement of a water heater or storage tank requires a permit. First confirm that the property is under the City of Los Angeles authority.

Is seismic strapping normally part of the scope?

Los Angeles guidance identifies seismic strapping as a usual requirement for water-heater work. The existing installation still needs to be reviewed.

What matters in a condo or multifamily building?

Share the unit and mechanical-area access, shutoff coordination, stairs or elevators, common-area rules, and any owner or association scheduling constraints.

Can it be completed today?

Call before noon. Completion by evening may be possible only after route, equipment, access, site conditions, and permits are confirmed by phone.
