Does Orange County issue every water-heater permit?

No. Orange County contains incorporated cities and unincorporated areas. OC Development Services describes its residential process for unincorporated county property; incorporated cities generally use their own authority.

Is the mailing city enough to identify jurisdiction?

Not always. Use the complete property address to confirm whether a city or the county handles the project before relying on a permit path.
