# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sacramento-area homeowners whose water heater has failed or needs replacement. They often arrive without hot water, feel urgency, and need to quickly understand whether the company serves their location, replaces their type of unit, can respond the same day, and how to request service.

## Product Purpose

Generate qualified calls and service requests for water heater replacement in Sacramento and nearby service areas. The site should begin indexing for high-intent local searches and provide a scalable foundation for city and equipment-specific landing pages.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, direct, calm. The voice should feel like a focused local specialist who explains the next step clearly and never pressures the homeowner.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded generic plumber websites, fake review counters, fabricated credentials, exaggerated emergency language, coupon-heavy layouts, and luxury styling that feels disconnected from an urgent home-service decision. Do not claim licensing, warranties, years in business, ratings, or customer testimonials until verified details are supplied.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next step obvious: phone and request form are immediately available.
2. Match urgent search intent with calm clarity, not alarmist sales copy.
3. Build trust through specificity about service, equipment types, process, and Sacramento coverage.
4. Give every section a search or conversion purpose so the page remains useful rather than padded.
5. Create a repeatable content structure for future city and equipment landing pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, high color contrast, descriptive form labels, clear validation, and comfortable tap targets for mobile visitors.
